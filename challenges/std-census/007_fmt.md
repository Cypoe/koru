# std/fmt — 823 lines for 5 tors; the mass is transform bodies

`fmt.kz` alone, no `~part` split. The taxonomy grew a class on this organ:
`fmt.dealloc` has zero call sites AND no transform emits it by name — it is
inserted by **auto-discharge** when a `FormattedText<!allocated>` obligation
needs a disposer (fmt.kz:47 says so; `continuation_codegen.zig` comments
document the insert). Call it **obligation-target**: reachable only through
the discharge machinery, live by construction, invisible to both layers of
the earlier census.

## Seams

- **`blk` in two packages.** `io:print.blk` (194 sites — formats AND prints)
  vs `fmt:fmt.blk` (11 sites — formats to an owned `FormattedText`). Real
  division of labor, but the shared `blk` suffix hides that one is a
  producer and one a sink. **task** — naming note; the census can't say
  which spelling should win, only that they read as siblings and aren't.
- **`[norun]` impls are `pub` again** — same seam as io's `print.impl`:
  shape-check internals exported at surface rank. Now confirmed as a
  *pattern*, not an io quirk — two organs, same costume problem.
  **task** — one ruling covers both: `[norun]` is the marker; what it needs
  is a `pub`-invisibility story.
- **The aspirational `fmt.blk.with_allocator` lives in a comment** (fmt.kz:348)
  — the same commented-FUTURE pattern as fs's handle API: designed surface
  parked as prose. Harmless, and worth knowing the pattern recurs.
  **task** — none alone; it joins the "commented futures" class.

## Frame feedback

The four-class taxonomy (`live / transform-target / internal / dead`) missed
**obligation-target** — tors the discharge machinery calls. Any tor carrying
a `!`-suffixed phantom param (`<!allocated>`, `<!instance>`) is a candidate:
zero call sites is its *healthy* state. Future entries should grep for
`<!...>` params before writing `dead`.

## Verdict

Designed — five tors, each with exactly one job and an honest ownership
story; the only real finding is that the census itself needed a fifth
classification this organ taught it.
