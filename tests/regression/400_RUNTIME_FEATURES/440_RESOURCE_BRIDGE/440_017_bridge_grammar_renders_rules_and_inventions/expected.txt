defined shout
A wire turn is one flow: verb(field: "value", field: "value") with `|` arms chaining what each call issues.
- The verb is a bare name from your vocabulary — letters, digits, '-', '_' — immediately followed by '('.
- Every value is a double-quoted string; escape quotes and backslashes inside (\", \\). No bare values.
- A second invocation starts a second item: one invocation per line runs each in order with one line of report per step.
- Prose is not wire. To speak, call the vocabulary's voice verb; to act, call its verb. Nothing else parses.
- Chain fresh outcomes with arms — a `| branch name |>` arm binds `name` to what the call just issued. Never invent one, never predict one: bind it.

Your vocabulary:
echo(text: string)
shout(text: string)
