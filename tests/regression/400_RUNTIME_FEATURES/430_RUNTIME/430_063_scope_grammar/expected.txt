Your reply IS wire — open it with an invocation, never narration: verb(field: value, field: value) with `|` arms chaining what each call issues.
- An arm binds what a call issued to a name for the next call: `| ok u |>` for an ordinary result, `| chosen c |>` for a named branch — the arm word is the branch name the verb's line in the vocabulary declares; a `| ?` outcome may go unarmed, every other named outcome must be armed or the turn stops with `unhandled-branch`.
- The verb is a bare name from your vocabulary — letters, digits, '-', '_' — immediately followed by '('.
- A value is a double-quoted string (escape \" and \\ inside) or a bare literal: a number, true/false, or a name a `|` arm already bound.
- A field typed `Expression` takes expression text bare (`calc(expr: 2 + 3)`); a field typed `Source` carries text — quote it: `note(text: "...")`, `say(text: "...")`. Bare prose ends early at a top-level `,` or `)` — a sentence with punctuation belongs in "quotes"; a verb whose only field is Source also answers to `verb: <rest of that line>`.
- A field typed `name<x>` takes only a bound name the session minted `<x!>` — an `rN` from an earlier verb or an arm binding, never literal text. `name<!x>` spends it.
- A bound name keeps its fields: `r3.user_id` reads that field out of the value r3 bound — the whole result stays readable in the session, not just its name.
- A second invocation starts a second item: one invocation per line runs each in order — to think out loud, lead with note(text: ...) and put the call that acts on the next line.
- Prose is not wire — it binds as residue: "residue bound: rN — your reply was not wire; nothing reached the user" keeps YOUR reply at rN — and a reply that is only prose is a turn spent reaching nobody.
- `<open>` — minted by open(...) | ok, append(...) | ok; spent by append(handle:), close(handle:)
- Chain fresh outcomes with arms: `open(path: "1") | ok v |> append(handle: v, text: "2")` — the arm's binding `v` is what the call just issued. Never invent one, never predict one: bind it.
